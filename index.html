Rules, controls & a little helpful moonlight

Tap a lantern to flip it and its four orthogonal neighbors. Your goal is to light them all. In later gardens, touching a violet soul-linked lantern also flips its paired lantern once. The rule applies only to the lantern you touch. Undo and restart are always available. The shortest route is calculated for the starting puzzle; hints adapt to your current board. All twelve gardens and daily puzzles are generated from solved states and verified by a solver.

Keyboard: arrow keys to choose a lantern, Enter or Space to touch it. U to undo, R to restart, H for a hint. Sound, language and completed gardens are saved on this device.
